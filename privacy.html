Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shonen AI collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Shonen AI Android app and related services.

1. Who we are

Shonen AI is an AI assistant app created to help users with chat, study, PDF tools, image assistance, productivity tools, and related AI features.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, Shonen AI may collect or process the following information:

  • Account information: email address, Firebase user ID, Google sign-in profile information if you choose Google login.
  • Chat and prompt data: messages, prompts, questions, and AI responses needed to provide AI chat features.
  • Uploaded content: images, PDF files, text, or documents you upload for AI tools, PDF tools, or image features.
  • App usage information: premium status, feature usage, app version, build number, update checks, and basic technical logs.
  • Support and feedback: bug reports, feedback messages, support requests, and contact details you provide.
  • Payment or premium activation information: information required to confirm premium access or support payment-related issues.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide AI chat, PDF tools, image tools, voice features, and productivity features.
  • Authenticate users and manage accounts.
  • Save user settings, app state, premium access, and feature availability.
  • Improve app reliability, fix bugs, and provide support.
  • Check app version and show update information.
  • Prevent abuse, misuse, spam, and unauthorized access.

4. AI processing

When you send prompts, messages, images, PDFs, or other content to Shonen AI, that content may be processed by our backend and by third-party AI service providers used to generate responses. Do not upload highly sensitive personal, financial, medical, legal, or confidential information unless you understand the risks.

5. Third-party services

Shonen AI may use third-party services to operate the app, including but not limited to:

  • Firebase for authentication, database, and app services.
  • Render or other hosting providers for backend hosting.
  • AI model providers for generating AI responses.
  • Payment or support services if premium features are used.
  • GitHub Pages for website hosting and APK release downloads.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share or process data with service providers only when needed to operate Shonen AI, provide AI responses, authenticate accounts, host services, process premium features, maintain security, or comply with legal requirements.

7. Data storage and retention

Some information may be stored to keep your account, settings, chat features, premium status, support history, or app functionality working. Uploaded files or prompts may be stored temporarily or processed as needed for the feature. We aim to keep data only as long as reasonably necessary for app functionality, security, support, and legal reasons.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data, including authentication, backend environment variables for secrets, and access controls where applicable. However, no internet-based service can guarantee 100% security.

9. User choices and deletion

You may contact us to request help with account deletion, data deletion, premium issues, or privacy questions. Some data may need to be retained for security, legal, backup, or abuse-prevention reasons.

10. Children’s privacy

Shonen AI is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to use online services without parental consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Shonen AI changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
