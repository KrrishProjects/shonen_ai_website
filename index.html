Privacy

Privacy & Security

Shonen AI is designed with privacy and security in mind. The app uses Firebase Authentication for Google sign-in and Firestore for saving user-specific data such as chats, profile memory, usage limits, and app preferences.

Your AI requests are sent through a secure backend hosted on Render. Sensitive keys such as Gemini API keys, fallback AI provider keys, Razorpay Key Secret, and payment secrets are never stored inside the Android APK.

Shonen AI may store chat history and profile memory only to provide a better personalized experience across sessions and devices. Profile Memory is used to improve study, creator, business, and career-related outputs.

Users can sign out, clear chats, delete profile memory, and manage app data from inside the app. More detailed data deletion options will be added as Shonen AI moves closer to public launch.

Payment processing is handled through Razorpay. Shonen AI does not store card numbers, UPI PINs, bank passwords, or sensitive payment credentials.

AI responses may be processed by trusted AI providers through the backend. Shonen AI uses provider fallback only to improve reliability and reduce downtime. User-facing secrets are not exposed.
